## Problem Statement

The HR Analytics Dashboard helps organizations **understand workforce dynamics** and identify key drivers of employee satisfaction, retention, and performance.
It provides a consolidated view of demographics, attrition patterns, performance metrics, and compensation, enabling HR leaders to make **data-driven decisions**.

Key business questions addressed include:

* What is the overall employee distribution across departments, roles, and demographics?
* What are the main factors driving **attrition**?
* How are employees performing across roles and experience levels?
* What patterns exist in **salary distribution** and workforce diversity?

---

## Steps Followed

* **Step 1** : HR dataset loaded into Tableau Public. Data cleaning was performed (removing nulls, correcting formats, handling categorical variables).
* **Step 2** : Created calculated fields to segment employees by **age groups, experience bands, and salary ranges**.
* **Step 3** : Built filters (Department, Gender, Education, Job Role, Years at Company) to allow interactive exploration.
* **Step 4** : Used **KPIs and card visuals** to highlight:

  * Total Employees
  * Attrition Rate
  * Average Age
  * Average Salary
* **Step 5** : Created visualizations:

  * Bar/Stacked Charts → Department-wise employee count & attrition
  * Donut Chart → Gender ratio and diversity distribution
  * Line Chart → Attrition vs. Years of Service
  * Heat Map → Salary vs. Performance rating trends
* **Step 6** : Incorporated interactivity using **filters, highlight actions, and drill-downs**.
* **Step 7** : Applied modern Tableau themes and formatting for clear insights and storytelling.
* **Step 8** : Published the dashboard to **Tableau Public** for easy access and sharing.

## Insights

Based on the dashboard, the following insights were derived:

### [1] Workforce Composition

* Majority of employees are concentrated in a few key departments (e.g., Sales, HR, R&D).
* Gender distribution indicates a **slight imbalance**, with more male employees compared to female employees.
* Most employees fall within the **25–40 age range**, showing a relatively young workforce.

### [2] Attrition Analysis

* Overall attrition rate is significant in **entry-level and mid-level roles**.
* Employees with **lower salary brackets and fewer years of experience** show higher attrition.
* Certain departments (like Sales and HR) exhibit above-average attrition compared to others.

### [3] Performance Trends

* Performance ratings are generally higher among employees with **5+ years of service**.
* Employees in senior roles have **higher stability and lower attrition**.
* High-performing employees tend to receive **better salary increments**, reducing attrition risk.

### [4] Salary Distribution

* Salaries are skewed towards mid-range employees, with fewer in high-salary bands.
* Pay disparity exists across departments; **technical roles show higher average salaries** compared to support functions.

### [5] Education & Experience Insights

* Employees with **higher education (Master’s, PhD)** show lower attrition and higher performance stability.
* Employees with **less than 3 years of experience** are most likely to leave.

---

## Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides organizations with **actionable insights** into workforce composition, attrition risks, performance distribution, and salary patterns.
By leveraging this tool, HR leaders can:

* Identify departments at risk of high attrition.
* Align compensation strategies with retention needs.
* Develop targeted employee engagement programs.
* Optimize workforce planning for long-term growth.

This Tableau dashboard acts as a **strategic decision-support system** for human resource management.
